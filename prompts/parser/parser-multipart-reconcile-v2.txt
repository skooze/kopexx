You are given the complete set of human-readable documents that make up one filing submitted to
the United States Securities and Exchange Commission, the plan you produced for parsing it, and a
compact inventory of every part that has been produced against that plan.

Your task is to decide whether the plan has actually been carried out, and to say what is still
required.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

Each source artifact is introduced by a line reading SOURCE ARTIFACT followed by its filename, and
then the artifact itself, exactly as it was filed.

You also receive a RECONCILIATION BRIEF containing, for every part of the plan: its identifier, its
title, the status the producing call declared, how many nodes it returned, how many of its source
references were located in the filing and how many were not, what it declared unresolved, and
whether it truncated.

The brief is an inventory, not the parses themselves. It is deliberately compact so that this call
has room to think about the whole filing rather than re-reading every response. Where a count looks
wrong to you, say so; do not assume it is right merely because it is a number.

THE CURRENT EFFECTIVE STATE

The brief carries a further section stating where the parse stands against the source itself, in
three lists:

    table elements still unaccounted
        element ids from the mechanical table inventory that no part reproduced, classified or
        declared unresolved

    source regions no claim reaches
        stretches of a member that fall outside every coverage claim every part made, each given
        as the member filename and the text at either end of the stretch

    images unaccounted
        filed images that no part referenced, classified or declared unresolved

These are MECHANICAL findings. They were produced by counting and searching, not by reading.
Nothing that produced them knows what a filing contains, what any section means, or which material
belongs where. That is the judgement being asked of you.

So do not treat a finding as an instruction. A table element nobody accounted for may be a
financial statement that was skipped, or a layout scaffold with no disclosure in it at all. A
region no claim reaches may be a page of narrative nobody read, or the whitespace and rendering
markup between two sections. Say which, and why.

Confirming that a finding is wrong is an accepted answer, and it is a useful one. If a region the
brief lists as unreached is in fact covered by a part whose anchors did not resolve, say so and
say which part: the region was read, and what failed was the anchor, which is a different repair.

WHAT TO RETURN

Return exactly one YAML 1.2 document and nothing else.

Do not put your response in a code fence. Do not begin with three backtick characters. Do not begin
with the word yaml. Do not write a sentence before the document or after it. The very first
character you emit must be the letter p, from the word parse_plan_id, which is the first key of the
document.

Wrap a value in double quotes whenever it contains a colon, a hash, an angle bracket, a leading
dash or bracket, a line break, or leading or trailing spaces, and escape any double quote inside it
as a backslash followed by a double quote. When in doubt, quote it. Quote every identifier and
every date.

SERIALIZING WHAT YOU COPIED

This document carries element ids, member filenames and short quotes out of the filing. Each case
below is written with the form that parses.

    A colon followed by a space turns a plain scalar into a mapping. Quote it.

        wrong    what: Item 8: Financial Statements
        right    what: "Item 8: Financial Statements"

    A leading hyphen followed by a space turns a value into a list item. Quote it. A hyphen
    inside a value is harmless but quote it too rather than deciding case by case.

        wrong    what: - Signatures
        right    what: "- Signatures"
        right    what: "Ten-year summary"

    An element id contains a hash character, which begins a comment in a plain scalar. Always
    quote an element id.

        wrong    source_table_id: aapl-20241228.htm#t13
        right    source_table_id: "aapl-20241228.htm#t13"

    A double quote inside a double-quoted value is escaped with a backslash.

        right    what: "the \"Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements\""

    An apostrophe or single quote needs no escaping inside a double-quoted value. Do not switch
    the value to single quotes to avoid it.

        right    what: "the Company's fiscal 2024 fourth quarter"

    A line break inside a value goes in a block scalar, never inside a quoted scalar. Write a
    vertical bar and indent the value on the lines below it.

        right    note: |
                   The consolidated statements of operations
                   and the notes attached to them

    A tab character inside a value is written as a backslash followed by the letter t, inside
    double quotes. Never indent any line of your response with a tab: YAML forbids a tab in
    indentation and the document stops parsing at that line.

        right    what: "Net sales\tby segment"

    A Unicode dash, a curly quote or any other non-ASCII character is copied through unchanged,
    inside double quotes. Do not replace an en dash or em dash with a hyphen, do not straighten a
    curly quotation mark, and do not convert a non-breaking space to a space. Quotes are compared
    to the preserved bytes; a tidied character is a character that is not there.

        right    what: "Three months ended — the “Successor” period"

THE DOCUMENT

parse_plan_id
    the identifier of the plan, copied exactly, quoted

cycle
    an integer, the reconciliation cycle you were told this is

plan_complete
    true only when you believe every human-readable thing in this filing is now represented by
    some part, or is listed in unresolvable below

missing
    a list of material in the filing that no part covers, each with
      where        where in the source it appears, as precisely as you can say
      what         what it appears to be
      why          why you believe no part covers it

uncovered_regions
    your judgement on each region the brief lists as reached by no claim, each with
      member       the member filename
      what         what you find in that stretch of the source
      verdict      one of: uncovered, covered_by_a_part_whose_anchors_failed, no_disclosure
      why          the reasoning, and for the middle verdict the part_id that covered it

unaccounted_tables
    your judgement on each table element the brief lists as unaccounted, each with
      source_table_id   the element id, quoted
      verdict           one of: request_a_table_call, classify_here, unresolvable
      classification    your own words for what the element is, when the verdict is classify_here
      classification_evidence
                        a short verbatim quote from the source supporting that classification, in
                        double quotes, when the verdict is classify_here
      why               the reasoning

unaccounted_images
    your judgement on each image the brief lists as unaccounted, each with
      filename     the image filename
      verdict      one of: assign_to_a_part, no_content_to_represent, unresolvable
      why          the reasoning, naming the part_id when you are assigning it

table_requests
    a list of the element ids you want asked about directly, quoted. Each becomes its own call,
    with this same complete filing and that one element in front of you. Request one when the
    element carries data you believe belongs in the parse and no part is going to reach it.

duplicated
    a list of material you believe more than one part covers, each with
      what         what the material is
      parts        the part_id values that cover it
      note         which one should own it, in your view

conflicting
    a list of places where two parts say incompatible things, each with
      what         what the disagreement is about
      parts        the part_id values that disagree
      note         what you would need in order to resolve it

additional_parts
    an ordered list of NEW parts required to cover what is missing. Each entry has
      part_id      a short identifier you choose, unique, quoted, and different from every
                   part_id already in the plan
      order        an integer
      title        what the filing calls that material
      type         what kind of material it is
      purpose      one sentence on what that part will contain
      expected_table_elements
                   a list of the element ids that part will account for, each id quoted
      expected_images
                   a list of the image filenames that part will account for

replacement_parts
    an ordered list of parts that must be produced again because the existing result cannot stand.
    Same fields as additional_parts. Give each a NEW part_id; the earlier result is kept as
    evidence and is not overwritten.

unresolvable
    a list of material that cannot be completed at all, each with what it is and why. Material you
    list here is reported to a person as unresolved. That is an acceptable outcome and is far
    better than describing the filing as covered when it is not.

model_declared_coverage
    two or three sentences stating, in your own words, what you believe is and is not covered

metadata
    anything else worth recording

WHAT plan_complete MEANS AND DOES NOT MEAN

It is your claim about your own work. It is recorded as your claim, shown to a person as your
claim, and it does not by itself mark anything complete. Coverage is separately checked by locating
the quotes each part supplied inside the filing's own bytes, and a person reviews the result.

So there is nothing to gain by claiming completeness you do not believe. A false complete is the
one outcome this whole process exists to prevent; an honest missing entry is the outcome it exists
to produce.

A REGION NOBODY CLAIMED IS THE HARDEST THING TO SEE

Every other finding here starts from something a part said. A region no claim reaches starts from
the source: it is material that produced no quote to check, no node to count and no error to
report, because nothing was ever written about it. It is invisible to every measurement except a
denominator taken from the filing itself, which is what the current effective state is.

Give each of those regions the same attention as a failed reference, and more than a duplicate.
A duplicate costs a reconciliation. A region nobody claimed is a hole in the parse that no later
step will find on its own.

ASKING FOR MORE WORK IS NORMAL

Every part you list in additional_parts or replacement_parts becomes another call, with this same
complete filing in front of you again, and so does every element id in table_requests. There is a
limit on how many reconciliation cycles run, and you are told which cycle this is. If material
still cannot be covered when the limit is reached, it is reported unresolved rather than quietly
dropped.

Do not return parsed content here. Do not reproduce a table, quote passages at length, or describe
what a section says beyond what is needed to identify it. This call returns a judgement about
coverage and a list of work.
