WHAT A COVERAGE CLAIM NEEDS FROM YOU

A coverage claim carries a start_anchor and an end_anchor, and each is a quote copied out of the
filing so that it can be searched for in the preserved bytes. Reproduce both character for
character, with their original spacing, punctuation, dashes and quotation marks.

An anchor you tidied is an anchor that will not be found, and a claim whose anchors are not found
bounds nothing. Correcting the quoting around an anchor is the repair. Correcting the anchor is
the loss of a region.

WHAT A TABLE GRID NEEDS FROM YOU

A structured table is a grid, and a grid survives only if every position in it survives.

    Do not renumber a row or a column. If a cell carries an explicit row or column integer, keep
    that integer. If it does not, keep the order the rows and the cells are written in, because
    that order is what the position is taken from.
    Do not drop a cell whose text is empty. An empty cell holds a position, and every cell after
    a dropped one moves into the wrong column.
    Do not merge two cells that look like one value split in two, and do not split one cell that
    looks like two values. Either change alters which row and which column a number sits in.
    Do not reorder rows, and do not move a row between header_rows, rows and footer_rows.
    Do not alter a row_span or a column_span, and do not remove a span you think is redundant.
    Keep source_table_id, source_anchor_start and source_anchor_end exactly as they are. They are
    what ties the grid to the bytes it came from.
    Keep the text of every cell character for character, with its commas, its parentheses, its
    currency and percentage marks, its sign and its footnote markers. Do not normalise a number

A grid that parses but has shifted by one column is worse than a document that fails to parse:
the failure is visible and the shift is not.

A value that must contain line breaks goes in a block scalar instead: write a vertical bar, then

Quote every identifier, every date, and every value that begins with a zero or looks like a number

THE CORRECTIONS, ONE BY ONE

Each case below is written with the broken form and the form that parses. The right-hand form
carries exactly the same characters as the left-hand one.

    A colon followed by a space turns a plain scalar into a mapping. Quote it.

        wrong    text: Total net sales: 391,035
        right    text: "Total net sales: 391,035"

    A leading hyphen followed by a space turns a value into a list item. Quote it. A hyphen
    inside a value, such as a negative number or a hyphenated word, is harmless but quote it too
    rather than deciding case by case.

        wrong    text: - 1,234
        right    text: "- 1,234"
        right    text: "(1,234) year-over-year"

    An element id contains a hash character, which begins a comment in a plain scalar. Always
    quote an element id.

        wrong    source_table_id: aapl-20241228.htm#t13
        right    source_table_id: "aapl-20241228.htm#t13"

    A double quote inside a double-quoted value is escaped with a backslash. Do not delete the

        wrong    text: "the "Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements""
        right    text: "the \"Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements\""

    An apostrophe or single quote needs no escaping inside a double-quoted value. Do not switch
    the value to single quotes to avoid it, and do not remove the apostrophe.

        right    text: "the Company's fiscal 2024 fourth quarter"

    A line break inside a quoted scalar does not parse. Move the value into a block scalar: write
    a vertical bar and indent the value on the lines below it, keeping the line breaks where they
    were.

        wrong    text: "Net sales
                 (in millions)"
        right    text: |
                   Net sales
                   (in millions)

    A tab character inside a value is written as a backslash followed by the letter t, inside
    double quotes. Never indent any line with a tab: YAML forbids a tab in indentation and the

    becomes spaces; a tab that was inside a value stays a tab, written as the escape.

        right    text: "Net sales\t1,234"

    A Unicode dash, a curly quote or any other non-ASCII character is carried through unchanged,
    inside double quotes. Do not replace an en dash or em dash with a hyphen, do not straighten a
