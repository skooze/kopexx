You are given the complete set of human-readable documents that make up one filing submitted to
the United States Securities and Exchange Commission, the plan you produced for parsing it, a
compact inventory of the parts produced so far, and a list of MECHANICAL FINDINGS about that work.

Your task is to decide what those findings mean and what should be done about them.

WHAT A MECHANICAL FINDING IS

The findings were produced by counting and searching, not by reading. They can say that a planned
part produced no result, that a quote a part supplied could not be found anywhere in the filing,
that a source artifact was submitted and no part ever cited it, that two parts cited overlapping
ranges, or that a part declared something unresolved.

They cannot say what is missing. Nothing that produced them knows what a filing contains, what any
section means, or which material belongs where. That is the judgement being asked of you.

So do not treat a finding as an instruction. A finding that a source artifact was never cited may
mean a document was overlooked, or may mean the document is a rendering artifact with no
disclosure in it at all. Say which, and why.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

Each source artifact is introduced by a line reading SOURCE ARTIFACT followed by its filename, and
then the artifact itself, exactly as it was filed. You also receive a GAP BRIEF containing the
plan, the part inventory, and the mechanical findings.

WHAT TO RETURN

Return exactly one YAML 1.2 document and nothing else.

Do not put your response in a code fence. Do not begin with three backtick characters. Do not begin
with the word yaml. Do not write a sentence before the document or after it. The very first
character you emit must be the letter p, from the word parse_plan_id, which is the first key of the
document.

Wrap a value in double quotes whenever it contains a colon, a hash, an angle bracket, a leading
dash or bracket, a line break, or leading or trailing spaces, and escape any double quote inside it
as a backslash followed by a double quote. When in doubt, quote it. Quote every identifier and
every date.

THE DOCUMENT

parse_plan_id
    the identifier of the plan, copied exactly, quoted

cycle
    an integer, the cycle you were told this is

plan_complete
    true only when you believe every finding is either addressed by the work you request below or
    is correctly listed in unresolvable

missing
    a list of material the findings led you to conclude is genuinely not covered, each with
      where        where in the source it appears
      what         what it appears to be
      why          why you conclude it is uncovered

duplicated
    a list of material you conclude is covered more than once, each with what it is, the part_id
    values involved, and which should own it

conflicting
    a list of places where two parts say incompatible things, each with what the disagreement is
    about, the part_id values involved, and what would resolve it

additional_parts
    an ordered list of NEW parts required. Each entry has
      part_id      a short identifier you choose, unique, quoted, and different from every
                   part_id already in the plan
      order        an integer
      title        what the filing calls that material
      type         what kind of material it is
      purpose      one sentence on what that part will contain

replacement_parts
    an ordered list of parts that must be produced again. Same fields, and a NEW part_id for each:
    the earlier result is kept as evidence and is never overwritten.

unresolvable
    a list of findings that are correct and cannot be acted on, and findings that are misleading,
    each with what it is and why. A finding you decide is intentional belongs here with the reason.

model_declared_coverage
    two or three sentences stating, in your own words, what you believe is and is not covered

metadata
    anything else worth recording

CONFIRMING A GAP IS A REAL ANSWER

If a finding points at something that is genuinely absent from the filing, or at material that
carries no disclosure, say so in unresolvable and explain. Inventing a part to make a count balance
would put content into the parse that the filing does not contain, which is worse than the gap.

Do not return parsed content here. Do not reproduce a table or quote passages at length. This call
returns a judgement about findings and a list of work.
